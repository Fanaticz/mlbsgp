Points per game in each quarter (overtime excluded). With Opp-adj on, every game is corrected for who it was against, quarter by quarter: scoring 30 in Q2 against a top Q2 defense counts for more than 30 against a bad one. The ratings are solved jointly for each season, so a team's numbers aren't skewed by a schedule full of good (or bad) opponents. Early in a season they're pulled toward average until there are enough games. Focus edge is the team's net in the focus quarter minus its average net in the other three, so a team that's +1 every quarter shows 0; a team that's −1 in Q1/Q3/Q4 but +3 in Q2 (the bench quarter) shows +4. Off / Def edge split that into scoring vs. defense. W-L is the team's actual record in the focus quarter (ties left out, never adjusted). Colors are relative to the league average for that quarter.
