Fair odds on who makes the game's first dunk. Each player's rate is his dunks per minute (last season counts three quarters, and a thin sample leans on his position's average), scaled by how many dunks the other team allows and by when he is usually on the floor as a starter or off the bench. Set each game's starters before trusting a number: a man who starts is worth several times the same man off the bench. EV is fair chance × the price − 1.
